# CALC-002 — Sodium-22 Decay Energetics

$$
{}^{22}Na\rightarrow{}^{22}Ne
$$

Atomic masses give:

$$
\boxed{
Q_{EC}\approx2.84320\ {\rm MeV}
}
$$

Ground-state positron-emission allowance:

$$
\boxed{
Q_{\beta^+,gs}
\approx1.82120\ {\rm MeV}
}
$$

However, the dominant decay populates the approximately:

$$
1274.537\ {\rm keV}
$$

excited state of ²²Ne.

Therefore the familiar β⁺ branch has a much smaller positron endpoint scale:

$$
1.82120-1.274537
\approx
0.54666\ {\rm MeV}
$$

before small recoil corrections.

Important rule:

```text
TOTAL DECAY Q
!=
POSITRON ENDPOINT OF THE DOMINANT EXCITED-STATE BRANCH
```
