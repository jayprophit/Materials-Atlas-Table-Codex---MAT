# CALC-003 — Sodium-24 Beta-Minus Decay

$$
{}^{24}Na
\rightarrow
{}^{24}Mg+e^-+\bar\nu_e
$$

Atomic masses:

$$
M({}^{24}Na)=23.990962950\ u
$$

$$
M({}^{24}Mg)=23.985041697\ u
$$

For β⁻ decay:

$$
Q_{\beta^-}
=
[M_p-M_d]c^2
$$

$$
\boxed{
Q_{\beta^-}\approx5.51561\ {\rm MeV}
}
$$

This total Q value must not be confused with the electron endpoint for a particular branch because much of the decay proceeds through excited ²⁴Mg levels followed by gamma emission.
