# CALC-001 — Sodium-21 Beta-Plus Decay

$$
{}^{21}Na\rightarrow{}^{21}Ne+e^++\nu_e
$$

Using neutral atomic masses:

$$
M({}^{21}Na)=20.99765469\ u
$$

$$
M({}^{21}Ne)=20.993846685\ u
$$

Electron-capture mass difference:

$$
Q_{EC}
=
[M_p-M_d]c^2
$$

$$
\boxed{
Q_{EC}\approx3.54713\ {\rm MeV}
}
$$

For positron emission:

$$
Q_{\beta^+}
=
Q_{EC}-2m_ec^2
$$

$$
\boxed{
Q_{\beta^+}\approx2.52514\ {\rm MeV}
}
$$
