# Sodium-Potassium Pump

## Jens Christian Skou

Role:

```text
ION-PUMP-DISCOVERY
MEMBRANE-TRANSPORT-CONTRIBUTOR
BIOCHEMISTRY-PIONEER
```

In 1957 Skou identified Na⁺,K⁺-activated ATPase.

Canonical transport:

```text
3 Na+ OUT
2 K+ IN
1 ATP
```

The pump maintains the gradients used by channels and coupled transport systems.
