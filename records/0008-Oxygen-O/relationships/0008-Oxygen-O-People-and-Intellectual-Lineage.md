# Oxygen — People and Intellectual Lineage

## Carl Wilhelm Scheele

Role:

```text
EARLY-OXYGEN-ISOLATION
DISCOVERY-LINEAGE
EXPERIMENTAL-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Scheele produced Oxygen before Priestley's published report, probably by 1771.

His account appeared later.

MAT edge:

```text
SCHEELE
→ PRODUCES / OBSERVES
→ OXYGEN-RICH GAS
```

---

## Joseph Priestley

Role:

```text
OXYGEN-DISCOVERY-LINEAGE
FIRST-PUBLISHED-ISOLATION
GAS-CHEMISTRY-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Priestley prepared the gas in 1774 and published his observations.

He interpreted it within phlogiston theory as:

```text
DEPHLOGISTICATED AIR
```

MAT therefore separates:

```text
EXPERIMENTAL OBSERVATION
```

from:

```text
CORRECT THEORETICAL INTERPRETATION
```

---

## Antoine Lavoisier

Role:

```text
THEORETICAL-INTERPRETATION-CONTRIBUTOR
COMBUSTION-CHEMISTRY-PIONEER
NOMENCLATURE-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Lavoisier incorporated Oxygen into a new account of combustion and oxidation and introduced the name:

```text
OXYGEN
```

The historical belief that Oxygen was universally the acid-forming principle was later shown to be incorrect.

# Atmospheric Oxygen / Ozone

## Sydney Chapman

Role:

```text
ATMOSPHERIC-PHOTOCHEMISTRY-PIONEER
THEORETICAL-CONTRIBUTOR
OZONE-CYCLE-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Chapman's 1930 model formalized the photochemical Oxygen/Ozone system:

```text
O2 + photon → O
O + O2 + M → O3 + M
O3 + photon → O + O2
O + O3 → 2O2
```

MAT scientific-status note:

```text
CHAPMAN MECHANISM
=
FOUNDATIONAL MODEL
```

but:

```text
CHAPMAN-ONLY CHEMISTRY
!=
COMPLETE MODERN STRATOSPHERIC OZONE MODEL
```

because catalytic NOx, HOx, ClOx and other chemistry must also be included.

Source:

```text
SRC-000149
```

# Photosynthetic Oxygen Evolution

## Robert Hill

Role:

```text
PHOTOSYNTHESIS-EXPERIMENTAL-CONTRIBUTOR
OXYGEN-EVOLUTION-PIONEER
```

Hill showed that isolated chloroplasts can evolve Oxygen during the light reactions in the presence of suitable electron acceptors without simultaneous CO2 fixation.

This separated:

```text
LIGHT-DRIVEN ELECTRON TRANSFER / WATER OXIDATION
```

from:

```text
CARBON FIXATION
```

```text
SRC-000151
```
