Role:

```text
PTFE-DISCOVERY
POLYMER-CHEMISTRY-CONTRIBUTOR
MATERIALS-DISCOVERY-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Important MAT lesson:

```text

+

→
NEW MATERIAL
```

# Noble-Gas Fluorides

## Neil Bartlett

Role:

```text
NOBLE-GAS-CHEMISTRY-PIONEER
HIGH-OXIDATION-FLUORIDE-CONTRIBUTOR
THEORETICAL-EXPERIMENTAL-EXTENDER
```

In 1962 Bartlett connected the strong oxidizing behavior of PtF6 with xenon's ionization energetics and demonstrated stable xenon-containing compounds.

Historic conceptual route:

```text
O2 + PtF6
→ O2+[PtF6]-

similar ionization-energy reasoning

Xe + PtF6
→ xenon fluoride / fluoroplatinate products
```

Modern qualification:

```text
ORIGINAL PRODUCT
!=
ONE SIMPLE Xe+[PtF6]- CRYSTAL SPECIES
```

The discovery nevertheless overturned absolute noble-gas inertness.

Source:

```text
SRC-000185
```
