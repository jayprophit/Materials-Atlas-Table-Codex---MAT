| Be I    |  166.1478 nm | strong/persistent UV line                     | SRC-000005 |
| Be I    |  234.8610 nm | \(2s^2\,^1S_0 \rightarrow 2s2p\,^1P^\circ_1\) | SRC-000005 |
| Be I    |  249.4728 nm | neutral-Be UV line                            | SRC-000005 |
| Be I    |  265.0454 nm | neutral-Be UV line                            | SRC-000005 |

For the 234.8610 nm transition:

$$
\nu=\frac{c}{\lambda}
\approx1.2765\times10^{15}\ {\rm Hz}
$$

and:

$$
E=\frac{hc}{\lambda}
\approx5.2790\ {\rm eV}
$$

These are properties of one photon transition.

They are not:

```text
THE UNIVERSAL FREQUENCY OF BERYLLIUM
```
