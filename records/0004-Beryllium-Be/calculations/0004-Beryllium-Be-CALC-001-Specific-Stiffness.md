# CALC-001 — Beryllium Specific Young's Modulus

$$
S_E=\frac{E}{\rho}
$$

Using:

$$
E=287\times10^9\ {\rm Pa}
$$

$$
\rho=1848\ {\rm kg\,m^{-3}}
$$

gives:

$$
\boxed{
S_E
=
1.5530\times10^8\ {\rm m^2\,s^{-2}}
}
$$
