# CALC-002 — Beryllium-8 Decay Q-Value

$$
{}^8Be
\rightarrow
{}^4He+{}^4He
$$

Using:

$$
m({}^8Be)=8.005305102\ u
$$

$$
m({}^4He)=4.00260325413\ u
$$

$$
\Delta m
=
8.005305102
-
2(4.00260325413)
$$

$$
\Delta m
=
9.859374\times10^{-5}\ u
$$

Using:

$$
1u\,c^2=931.49410372\ {\rm MeV}
$$

gives:

$$
\boxed{
Q\approx0.0918395\ {\rm MeV}
}
$$
