# Beryllium — People and Intellectual Lineage

## René-Just Haüy

Role:

```text
HISTORICAL-PRECURSOR
MINERALOGICAL-IDENTIFICATION-LINEAGE
```

Haüy suspected that beryl and emerald contained an unknown constituent and encouraged chemical investigation.

---

## Louis Nicolas Vauquelin

Role:

```text
DISCOVERED-ELEMENT
CHEMICAL-IDENTIFICATION
```

In 1798 Vauquelin identified the new element in beryl/emerald material.

He did not isolate pure metallic Beryllium.

---

## Friedrich Wöhler

Role:

```text
INDEPENDENT-METAL-ISOLATION
```

Wöhler isolated metallic Beryllium in 1828 through reduction chemistry involving Beryllium chloride.

---

## Antoine Bussy

Role:

```text
INDEPENDENT-METAL-ISOLATION
```

Bussy independently isolated metallic Beryllium in 1828.

MAT therefore distinguishes:

```text
DISCOVERY / IDENTIFICATION
```

from:

```text
ISOLATION OF ELEMENTAL METAL
```

Role:


---

## James Chadwick

Role:

```text
DISCOVERED-NEUTRON
THEORETICAL-AND-EXPERIMENTAL-INTERPRETATION
```

Chadwick demonstrated in 1932 that the penetrating radiation was consistent with a neutral massive particle:

```text
NEUTRON
```

Relationship:

```text
9Be + alpha
↓
neutron-producing radiation
↓
experimental anomaly
↓
neutron identification
```

Beryllium therefore has a direct place in the experimental lineage of neutron discovery.
