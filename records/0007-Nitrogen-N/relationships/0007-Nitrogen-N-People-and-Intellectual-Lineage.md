# Nitrogen — People and Intellectual Lineage

## Henry Cavendish

Role:

```text
EXPERIMENTAL-PRECURSOR
GAS-CHEMISTRY-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Produced Nitrogen-rich residual gas through removal of reactive components from air.

He did not establish it as a new element.

---

## Joseph Priestley

Role:

```text
EXPERIMENTAL-PRECURSOR
PNEUMATIC-CHEMISTRY-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Also obtained gas depleted in oxygen and observed its inability to sustain ordinary combustion or animal respiration.

---

## Daniel Rutherford

Role:

```text
ELEMENT-IDENTIFICATION-CONTRIBUTOR
NITROGEN-DISCOVERY-LINEAGE
```

In 1772 Rutherford described the residual gas and recognized it as a distinct component of air.

MAT distinguishes:

```text
GAS OBTAINED
```

from:

```text
GAS RECOGNIZED AS DISTINCT SUBSTANCE/ELEMENT
```

Source:

```text
SRC-000120
```

# Industrial Nitrogen Fixation

## Fritz Haber

Role:

```text
AMMONIA-SYNTHESIS-PIONEER
CATALYTIC-CHEMISTRY-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Established practical laboratory synthesis:

$$
N_2+3H_2
\rightleftharpoons
2NH_3
$$

under elevated pressure and temperature.

Nobel Prize in Chemistry:

```text
1918
```

Source:

```text
SRC-000107
```

---

## Robert Le Rossignol

Role:

```text
EXPERIMENTAL-AND-INSTRUMENTATION-CONTRIBUTOR
AMMONIA-SYNTHESIS-TEAM
```

Worked with Haber on the laboratory apparatus used to demonstrate continuous ammonia synthesis.

---

## Carl Bosch

Role:

```text
INDUSTRIAL-SCALEUP-PIONEER
HIGH-PRESSURE-ENGINEERING-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Converted Haber’s laboratory chemistry into industrial high-pressure ammonia production.

Nobel Prize in Chemistry:

```text
1931
```

Source:

```text
SRC-000108
```

Role:

```text
CATALYST-DEVELOPMENT-CONTRIBUTOR
INDUSTRIAL-CHEMISTRY-EXTENDER
```
