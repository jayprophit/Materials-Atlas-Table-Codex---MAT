Role:

```text
EARLY-BORON-ISOLATION-LINEAGE
```

Role:

EARLY-BORON-ISOLATION-LINEAGE

## Humphry Davy

Role:

```text
INDEPENDENT-EARLY-BORON-ISOLATION
ELECTROCHEMICAL-CHEMISTRY-LINEAGE
```

Davy independently obtained Boron-containing elemental material in 1808.

# Borane and Multicentre-Bonding Lineage

## William N. Lipscomb

Role:

```text
BORANE-STRUCTURE-PIONEER
MULTICENTRE-BONDING-CONTRIBUTOR
TOPOLOGICAL-STRUCTURE-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Lipscomb and collaborators determined Borane structures and developed a deeper understanding of their unusual electron-deficient bonding.

His work showed that conventional two-centre/two-electron bonding alone could not adequately explain the Borane family.

He received the 1976 Nobel Prize in Chemistry:

```text
for studies of borane structures illuminating chemical bonding
```

MAT relationship:

```text
BORON ELECTRON COUNT
↓
MULTICENTRE BONDING
↓
POLYHEDRAL STRUCTURE
```

---

## Kenneth Wade

Role:

```text
THEORETICAL-EXTENDER
CLUSTER-ELECTRON-COUNTING
```

Wade developed electron-counting rules linking skeletal electron-pair counts to families of polyhedral cluster structures.

Canonical classes include:

```text
CLOSO
NIDO
ARACHNO
```

For a closo cluster with \(n\) vertices:

$$
N_{\rm SEP}=n+1
$$

skeletal electron pairs.

The rules are structural predictors and organizing principles.

They are not universal laws for every Boron-containing structure.

---

## D. Michael P. Mingos

Role:

```text
THEORETICAL-EXTENDER
CLUSTER-ELECTRON-COUNTING
```

Extended and generalized cluster electron-counting concepts across main-group and transition-metal cluster chemistry.

---

## Eluvathingal D. Jemmis

Role:

```text
THEORETICAL-EXTENDER
MACROPOLYHEDRAL-CLUSTER-RULES
```

Developed the `mno` framework for condensed/macropolyhedral cluster systems.

This extends the intellectual lineage from isolated polyhedra toward connected cluster architectures.
