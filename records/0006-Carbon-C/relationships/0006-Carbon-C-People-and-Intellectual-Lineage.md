# Carbon — People and Intellectual Lineage

## Antoine Lavoisier

Role:

```text
HISTORICAL-ELEMENTAL-IDENTITY-CONTRIBUTOR
COMBUSTION-CHEMISTRY-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Lavoisier's combustion studies helped establish Carbon as an elemental substance and connected diamond combustion with carbon dioxide formation.

---

## Smithson Tennant

Role:

```text
ALLOTROPE-IDENTITY-EXPERIMENTAL-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Tennant demonstrated that diamond and ordinary carbonaceous material produce the same Carbon dioxide product under combustion, strengthening the conclusion that diamond is Carbon.
