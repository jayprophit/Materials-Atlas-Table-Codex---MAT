# CALC-002 — Carbon-14 Beta Decay Q-Value

Reaction:

$$
{}^{14}C
\rightarrow
{}^{14}N
+
e^-
+
\bar{\nu}_e
$$

Using neutral atomic masses:

$$
m({}^{14}C)
=
14.0032419884\ u
$$

$$
m({}^{14}N)
=
14.00307400443\ u
$$

gives:

$$
\Delta m
=
0.00016798397\ u
$$

and:

$$
\boxed{
Q_\beta
\approx
0.1564761\ {\rm MeV}
}
$$

or approximately:

$$
156.476\ {\rm keV}
$$

This is the total beta-decay energy available to the decay products.

It is not the energy of every emitted electron.

The beta particles occupy a continuous energy spectrum because energy
and momentum are shared with the antineutrino and recoiling daughter.
