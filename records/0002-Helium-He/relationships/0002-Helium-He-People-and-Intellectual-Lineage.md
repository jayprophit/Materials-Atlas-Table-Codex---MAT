# Helium — People and Intellectual Lineage

## Pierre Janssen

Role:

```text
SPECTROSCOPIC-DISCOVERY-LINEAGE
```

Associated with observation of the solar spectral line that preceded terrestrial isolation of Helium.

---

## Norman Lockyer

Role:

```text
SPECTROSCOPIC-IDENTIFICATION / NAMING-LINEAGE
```

Interpreted the solar spectral feature as evidence for a previously unknown element.

---

## Edward Frankland

Role:

```text
NAMING-LINEAGE
```

Associated with the naming of Helium with Lockyer.

---

## William Ramsay

Role:

```text
TERRESTRIAL-ISOLATION / IDENTIFICATION
```

Linked to establishing Helium on Earth through gas released from mineral material.

---

## Per Teodor Cleve and Abraham Langlet

Role:

```text
INDEPENDENT-TERRESTRIAL-IDENTIFICATION
```

Part of the independent terrestrial Helium-identification lineage.

---

## Heike Kamerlingh Onnes

Role:

```text
FIRST-LIQUEFACTION-OF-HELIUM
CRYOGENIC-PIONEER
```

In 1908 Onnes successfully produced liquid Helium, opening a major new low-temperature regime. ([Nobel Prize][3])

---

## Pyotr Kapitza

Role:

```text
SUPERFLUID-HELIUM-4-EXPERIMENTAL-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Kapitza's experiments established the extraordinary flow behavior of He II and identified it as a superfluid. ([Nobel Prize][4])

---

## John F. Allen

Role:

```text
SUPERFLUID-HELIUM-4-EXPERIMENTAL-CONTRIBUTOR
```

---

## A. D. Misener

Role:

```text
SUPERFLUID-HELIUM-4-EXPERIMENTAL-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Allen and Misener independently reported the unusual flow behavior of liquid Helium II in the same historical period as Kapitza.

---

## Lev Landau

Role:

```text
THEORETICAL-QUANTUM-FLUID-CONTRIBUTOR
```

Developed the theoretical description of quantum collective excitations in liquid Helium, including the phonon/roton framework. ([Nobel Prize][5])

---

## Douglas D. Osheroff

DISCOVERED-SUPERFLUIDITY-IN-HELIUM-3

## Anthony J. Leggett

Role:

```text
THEORETICAL-EXTENDER
HELIUM-3-SUPERFLUID-THEORY
```

Linked to theoretical understanding of superfluid ³He and more general macroscopic quantum phenomena.

# Classification Rule

Do not collapse these roles into:

```text
DISCOVERED HELIUM
```

The lineage distinguishes:

```text
astronomical spectral discovery
terrestrial isolation
liquefaction
4He superfluidity
4He theory
3He superfluidity
3He theory
precision atomic science
nuclear science
```

These are different contributions.
