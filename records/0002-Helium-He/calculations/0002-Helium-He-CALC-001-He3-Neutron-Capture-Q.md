# CALC-001 — ³He Neutron-Capture Q-Value

Reaction:

$$
{}^3He+n
\rightarrow
{}^3H+{}^1H
$$

Using atomic masses:

$$
m({}^3He)=3.0160293201\ {\rm u}
$$

$$
m_n=1.00866491595\ {\rm u}
$$

$$
m({}^3H)=3.0160492779\ {\rm u}
$$

$$
m({}^1H)=1.00782503223\ {\rm u}
$$

The electron count balances when atomic masses are used.

Mass difference:

$$
\Delta m
=
0.00081992592\ {\rm u}
$$

Using:

$$
1\ {\rm u}c^2
=
931.49410372\ {\rm MeV}
$$

gives:

$$
\boxed{
Q\approx0.7637562\ {\rm MeV}
}
$$

This exothermic capture reaction underlies an important ³He neutron-detection relationship.
