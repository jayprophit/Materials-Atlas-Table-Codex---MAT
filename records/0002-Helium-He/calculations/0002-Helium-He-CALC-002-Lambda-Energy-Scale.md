# CALC-002 — ⁴He Lower Lambda-Point Thermal Energy Scale

Reference:

$$
T_\lambda
=
2.1768\ {\rm K}
$$

A characteristic thermal energy scale can be written:

$$
E_T=k_BT_\lambda
$$

This is a **thermal scale**, not the microscopic excitation spectrum itself.

Using the exact SI Boltzmann constant:

$$
k_B
=
1.380649\times10^{-23}\ {\rm J\,K^{-1}}
$$

gives:

$$
E_T
\approx
3.006\times10^{-23}\ {\rm J}
$$

The calculation must not be interpreted as:

```text
one quantum of superfluid helium energy
```

It is simply \(k_BT\) evaluated at the reference transition temperature.
