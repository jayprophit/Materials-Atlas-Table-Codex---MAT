# Lithium-Ion Battery Lineage

## M. Stanley Whittingham

Role:

```text
INTERCALATION-BATTERY-PIONEER
```

Contribution:

Developed an early rechargeable Lithium battery using a host structure capable of reversible Lithium insertion.

---

## John B. Goodenough

Role:

```text
HIGH-VOLTAGE-CATHODE-EXTENDER
```

Contribution:

Developed high-voltage Lithium-cobalt-oxide cathode chemistry, greatly increasing useful cell voltage.

---

## Akira Yoshino

Role:

```text
COMMERCIAL-LITHIUM-ION-ARCHITECTURE
```

Contribution:

Developed a practical rechargeable architecture using carbonaceous negative-electrode materials rather than metallic Lithium.

Relationship chain:

```text
Whittingham
↓
reversible Li insertion

Goodenough
↓
high-voltage oxide cathode

Yoshino
↓
commercially viable Li-ion architecture
```
