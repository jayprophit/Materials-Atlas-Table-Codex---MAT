# CALC-002 — Lithium-7 Tritium-Producing Reaction

$$
{}^7Li+n
\rightarrow
{}^4He+{}^3H+n
$$

Because the neutron appears on both sides:

$$
\Delta m
=
m({}^7Li)
-
m({}^4He)
-
m({}^3H)
$$

giving:

$$
\boxed{
Q=-2.467617\ MeV
}
$$

The negative value means the reaction is endothermic and requires sufficient incident energy.
