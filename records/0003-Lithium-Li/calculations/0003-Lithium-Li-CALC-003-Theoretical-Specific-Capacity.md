# CALC-003 — Lithium Metal Theoretical Specific Charge Capacity

For:

$$
Li\rightarrow Li^++e^-
$$

one mole of Lithium transfers one mole of electrons.

$$
Q_s
=
\frac{F}{3.6M}
$$

For:

$$
M=6.938\ g\,mol^{-1}
$$

$$
Q_s\approx3863\ mAh\,g^{-1}
$$

For:

$$
M=6.997\ g\,mol^{-1}
$$

$$
Q_s\approx3830\ mAh\,g^{-1}
$$

Thus:

$$
\boxed{
Q_s\approx3830-3863\ mAh\,g^{-1}
}
$$

across the MAT standard-atomic-weight interval.
