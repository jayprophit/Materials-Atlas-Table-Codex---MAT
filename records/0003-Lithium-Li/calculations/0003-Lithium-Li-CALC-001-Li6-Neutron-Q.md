# CALC-001 — Lithium-6 Neutron Reaction

$$
{}^6Li+n
\rightarrow
{}^4He+{}^3H
$$

Using atomic masses:

$$
m({}^6Li)=6.0151228874\ u
$$

$$
m_n=1.00866491595\ u
$$

$$
m({}^4He)=4.00260325413\ u
$$

$$
m({}^3H)=3.0160492779\ u
$$

$$
\Delta m
=
m({}^6Li)+m_n-m({}^4He)-m({}^3H)
$$

Using:

$$
1u\,c^2=931.49410372\ MeV
$$

gives:

$$
\boxed{
Q=4.783475\ MeV
}
$$
