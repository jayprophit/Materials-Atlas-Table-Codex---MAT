# Proposed element research catalogue: 119–188

The requested scope covers atomic numbers 1–188. **118 are recognised elements; 70 are proposed research entries.** The [IUPAC table](https://iupac.org/what-we-do/periodic-table-of-elements/) (SRC-000307, checked 26 September 2026) governs recognised scope. Foundation 0000 remains a separate information reference.

[Combined 0000–0188 coverage](../../data/quality/element-scope-0000-0188.json) · [Proposed index](../../data/catalog/proposed-elements-index.json) · [Shared state-flow profile](../../data/research/proposed-elements/state-flow-profile.json)

Systematic names follow the [IUPAC naming rules](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/AtWt/element.html) (SRC-000306). These derived identifiers do not establish existence, discovery, stability or a permanent approved name. Every entry has all 36 domain-review slots, the existing 652 state-flow requirements, and separate A01–A09/B01–B13 plans. They are **pending**, not completed datasets or images. No mass number, half-life, electron configuration, spectrum, crystal structure or bulk property has been invented.

Published predictions, author proposals and historical claims have separate collections. A new numerical prediction must carry a primary source, exact model and isotope/state, conditions, uncertainty and limitations. Do not extrapolate the Aufbau diagram or bulk properties indiscriminately, especially in the high-Z regime. A lack of data is UNKNOWN or INSUFFICIENT DATA; NOT APPLICABLE requires a scientific reason.
