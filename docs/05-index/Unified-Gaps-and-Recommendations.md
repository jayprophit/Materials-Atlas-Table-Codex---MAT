- Priority 1: Complete provenance and acceptance review before assigning a fully advanced status. All element-domain cells currently require scientific acceptance; source/heading presence is only discovery evidence.
- Priority 1: Keep one canonical owner per dataset and project, using versioned references for shared data. Peer desktop repositories have independent work in progress; a shared programme does not imply safe file-level merging.
- Priority 2: Finish Hydrogen as the reviewed publication example while expanding source-backed data batches across all applicable elements. The 9+13 standalone-image contract is only partially populated; mature authored records govern flow rather than generic placeholders.
- Priority 2: Prioritise missing nuclear Q-value review, isotope-specific NMR, actual spectral lines and state-specific solid/phase/material measurements. Existing nuclear and ionisation tables do not supply these distinct domains.
- Priority 2: Use source uncertainty and covariance for calculations; report unavailable uncertainty explicitly. Derived precision and energetically possible channels must not be presented as new measurements or observed decay.
- Priority 2: Separate author hypotheses, historical material and concept art from evaluated physical data. Cross-project conversation and image content may be useful design input without independent scientific support.
- Priority 3: Add typed shared envelopes for source, claim, state, artifact, task and acceptance evidence; validate a small round trip before broad integration. Shared provenance and status semantics can connect projects without claiming working integration.
