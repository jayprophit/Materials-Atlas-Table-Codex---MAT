# Materials, phenomena and classical elements

Water, earth, fire, air, electricity, ice, light, darkness, wood and metal are welcome in MAT. This collection gives each concept one canonical identity without assigning an invented atomic number. Sound is included as an adjacent frequency-related phenomenon.

Chemical elements, compounds, mixtures, material classes, processes and cultural concepts are different record classes. Related-element links are examples or constituents, not universal composition percentages. Numerical metrics require a defined sample, state or measurement; no universal frequency is supplied.

## Water

Canonical identifier: `MAT:REG:CONCEPT-WATER`. Class: COMPOUND.

Liquid water is a chemical compound; natural waters additionally contain dissolved and suspended substances.

Metrics to record: temperature K, pressure Pa, density kg/m3, pH with method, conductivity S/m, composition, vibrational spectrum.

Sources: SRC-000308, SRC-000318.

## Earth

Canonical identifier: `MAT:REG:CONCEPT-EARTH`. Class: MIXTURE-AND-CLASSICAL-CONCEPT.

Specify soil, rock, crust, whole planet or a cultural meaning; these are different systems.

Metrics to record: mineralogy, elemental composition, moisture, grain size, porosity, density, geographic provenance.

Sources: Detailed source review pending; introductory classification only..

## Fire

Canonical identifier: `MAT:REG:CONCEPT-FIRE`. Class: REACTION-PHENOMENON.

Combustion involves fuel, oxidiser, heat transfer and reaction chemistry. No single elemental identity or frequency describes every fire.

Metrics to record: fuel and oxidiser, heat release rate W, temperature K, emission spectrum, combustion products, mass loss rate kg/s.

Sources: SRC-000322.

## Air

Canonical identifier: `MAT:REG:CONCEPT-AIR`. Class: GAS-MIXTURE.

Atmospheric gas mixture; distinguish dry gas composition, humidity, aerosols, altitude and sampling date.

Metrics to record: mole fractions, pressure Pa, temperature K, humidity, aerosol concentration, sound speed m/s.

Sources: SRC-000320.

## Electricity

Canonical identifier: `MAT:REG:CONCEPT-ELECTRICITY`. Class: PHYSICAL-PHENOMENON.

Electric charge, fields and currents; properties depend on the circuit, medium and operating conditions.

Metrics to record: potential V, current A, field V/m, impedance ohm, power W, frequency Hz, waveform.

Sources: SRC-000323.

## Ice

Canonical identifier: `MAT:REG:CONCEPT-ICE`. Class: SOLID-PHASE-OF-COMPOUND.

Solid water; phase identity, temperature, pressure and impurities must be specified.

Metrics to record: phase, temperature K, pressure Pa, density kg/m3, lattice parameters, thermal conductivity W/(m K), latent heat J/kg.

Sources: SRC-000318.

## Light

Canonical identifier: `MAT:REG:CONCEPT-LIGHT`. Class: ELECTROMAGNETIC-RADIATION.

Visible light is part of the electromagnetic spectrum. In broader usage light can denote electromagnetic radiation beyond the visible band.

Metrics to record: frequency Hz, wavelength m with medium, spectral irradiance, polarisation, photon energy J, bandwidth, source spectrum.

Sources: SRC-000319.

## Darkness

Canonical identifier: `MAT:REG:CONCEPT-DARKNESS`. Class: OBSERVATION-CONDITION.

A low or absent visible-light condition relative to an observer or instrument; not a substance or negative-energy element.

Metrics to record: illuminance lx, spectral radiance, detector sensitivity, background signal, bandpass, adaptation conditions.

Sources: SRC-000319.

## Wood

Canonical identifier: `MAT:REG:CONCEPT-WOOD`. Class: BIOLOGICAL-COMPOSITE.

Plant-derived composite containing cellulose, hemicelluloses, lignin and other constituents; species, grain direction and moisture matter.

Metrics to record: species, moisture fraction, density kg/m3, grain direction, elastic moduli Pa, strength Pa, acoustic response, decay and process history.

Sources: SRC-000321.

## Metal

Canonical identifier: `MAT:REG:CONCEPT-METAL`. Class: MATERIAL-CLASS.

A class spanning metallic elements and alloys; choose an identified composition and phase before assigning properties.

Metrics to record: composition, phase, conductivity S/m, density kg/m3, elastic moduli Pa, yield stress Pa, corrosion conditions, magnetic response.

Sources: Detailed source review pending; introductory classification only..

## Sound

Canonical identifier: `MAT:REG:CONCEPT-SOUND`. Class: MECHANICAL-WAVE.

Mechanical disturbance propagated through a medium; distinguish frequency, sound pressure and medium response.

Metrics to record: frequency Hz, sound pressure Pa, level dB with reference, wave speed m/s, medium, attenuation.

Sources: SRC-000313.

## Cultural and discovery connections

Classical or cultural uses of these names may be catalogued with the tradition, author, date and text reference. A shared word does not imply that different traditions describe the same physical substance. No specific tradition has been assigned without source review.

These concepts link to material synthesis, environmental science, biology, engineering and frequency research. Bonding changes can create new compounds; nuclear identity changes are a separate discovery path. See [frequency comparisons and discovery](Frequency-Comparison-and-Discovery.md).
