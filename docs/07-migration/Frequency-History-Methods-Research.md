# Frequency, history and adjacent methods research

Continue to the [frequency comparison and discovery guide](Frequency-Comparison-and-Discovery.md), [source table](Spooky2-Frequency-Reference.md), and [materials, phenomena and classical elements](Materials-Phenomena-Classical-Elements.md). Sources SRC-000309, SRC-000310 and SRC-000311 now have separate provenance-bound imports.

This page preserves user-supplied leads about Royal Raymond Rife, Walter Russell frequency models, electroporation and gene-editing methods. It is a research index, not a conversion of those claims into MAT facts.

The Rife material is kept in the historical/alternative lane. Cancer Research UK reports that reliable evidence for Rife machines curing cancer is absent and that the machines have not undergone the usual scientific testing. Commercial Rife pages and frequency lists are retained as claims and context. They do not establish a therapeutic frequency, diagnosis, safety or efficacy.

Walter Russell’s periodic/frequency diagrams remain historical or alternative models. Their visual relationships may inform a separately labelled interface, but a symbolic frequency is not an atomic spectral measurement. A measured line requires a defined species and state, medium, transition, instrument/method and uncertainty.

The NEPA21, synthetic-DNA, CRISPR-antibody and gene-synthesis links are commercial method/equipment references. The relevant MAT record should preserve contributor, method, equipment, cell/material system, environment, protocol, controls, outcome and source locator for any future experiment. Product descriptions are not independent evidence of a biological result.

The machine-readable registry is [frequency-history-and-methods.yaml](../../data/research/frequency-history-and-methods.yaml). The user-supplied videos remain `UNREVIEWED-USER-SUPPLIED` until their titles, speakers, dates and claims are independently checked.
