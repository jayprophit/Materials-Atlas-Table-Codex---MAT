# MAT Glossary

## Allotrope

Different structural form of the same element.

---

## Anisotropy

Dependence of a property on direction.

---

## Atomic Number

Number of protons in an atomic nucleus.

---

## Causali E

The author's developing conceptual framework concerning causal change, retained constraints and potential/resulting states.

---

## Compound

Substance composed of two or more elements chemically combined in defined relationships.

---

## Confidence

MAT assessment of how securely a claim is supported.

Not identical to measurement uncertainty.

---

## Constraint

A condition restricting possible states or transformations.

---

## Data-Extruded Model

A three-dimensional MAT visualization whose geometry is generated from normalized data metrics.

It is not necessarily literal physical geometry.

---

## Evidence Type

Classification describing how a claim is supported.

Examples:

```text
MEASURED
REPLICATED
THEORETICAL
COMPUTATIONAL
HYPOTHESIS
```

---

## Isotope

Atoms with the same proton number but different neutron numbers.

---

## MAT

Materials Atlas Table Codex.

---

## Material State

A material under a defined set of composition, structure, environment, history, time and scale conditions.

---

## Measurement

Quantitative observation produced using a defined method.

---

## Metastable

A state that persists despite not being the lowest accessible energy state because of kinetic barriers.

---

## Nuclide

A nuclear species characterized by proton and neutron numbers and nuclear state.

---

## Phase

Physically distinguishable state of matter characterized by structural/thermodynamic properties.

---

## Process History

Sequence of treatments or environments experienced by a material.

---

## Provenance

Traceable history of where information originated and how MAT obtained or transformed it.

---

## Replication

Independent testing of a scientific result.

---

## Schema

Formal structure defining fields and relationships within MAT.

---

## State Space

Multidimensional mathematical space whose coordinates represent variables describing a system.

---

## Uncertainty

Quantitative or qualitative representation of limits associated with a measurement or prediction.

---
